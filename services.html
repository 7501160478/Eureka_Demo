Our Services

All medical facilities, Digital Central Monitoring and Expert opinion — complete diabetes care under one club.

01

All medical facilities

The tests and check-ups a diabetic patient needs, available in one place — so nothing is skipped and every result goes straight into your record.

  • Fasting & post-meal sugar
  • HbA1c
  • Lipid profile
  • Kidney function
  • Eye (retina) check-up
  • Foot examination
  • Blood pressure
  • Weight & BMI
Book a check-up
02

Digital Central Monitoring

Every reading of every member is kept in one central digital record. We follow the trend over time and act as soon as something moves out of range.

  • One digital record for every patient
  • Sugar trend chart with the target range
  • Flags for high, low or overdue readings
  • Full reading history at a glance

Expert opinion

Experienced doctors review your complete record — not just a single test — and guide you on what to change and why.

  • Review of your full reading history
  • Medication advice and adjustment
  • Diet and lifestyle guidance
  • Referral to specialists when needed
