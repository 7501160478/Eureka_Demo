About the club

Care that continues between doctor visits

Diabetes is managed every day, not only on the day of a check-up. The Diabetic Care Club by EUREKA keeps every member's health record up to date and watches it continuously, so problems are caught early and treatment stays on track.

  • One complete record for every patient
  • Out-of-range readings flagged early
  • Regular follow-up so no check-up is missed

Why the club

Our approach to diabetes care

Continuity of care

Care does not stop after a visit. We stay with our members through the whole year.

Complete records

Every test and reading is kept in one record, so the full picture is always available.

Early detection

Readings outside the target range are flagged early, before they turn into complications.

Expert guidance

Doctors review each record and give clear advice on medicine, diet and lifestyle.

How it works

Year-round care in four simple steps

  1. 1

    Enroll

    Join the club and get a baseline check-up with your full history recorded.

  2. 2

    Record readings

    Sugar, HbA1c, blood pressure and weight are logged at every visit.

  3. 3

    Continuous monitoring

    Trends are followed over time and out-of-range readings are highlighted.

  4. 4

    Expert review

    Doctors review the record and adjust your care plan whenever it is needed.
